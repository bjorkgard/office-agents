## Section findings

**S1 Architecture.** Door: `doorSpan` next to `paperOfParent` in `src/office/paper.ts` (reuses `leavingSpan`, `SubagentCtx`, `FADE_MS`; it needs `ctx` and `geo`, not just agent fields) returning `{open, nextChange}`; skipped for `ctx.resume` and re-planned or queued subagents and for subagents first seen on a snapshot or reload (nobody walks from the door); window end `min`'d with removal at `machine.ts:414`; Scene folds `nextChange` into `Math.min(nextPaper, nextDoor)` and the existing `armPaperTimer` (`Scene.tsx:319`), so no new timer. Robot: one absolutely placed element, CSS `@keyframes` on `transform` only, lane from `floorCorners(rows)`; parked frame under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)`. Wall dressing: new props in `props.ts`, entries in `WALL_LAYOUT` and `WALL_ANCHOR`, rendered in `RoomDecor.tsx`. Contact shadows: one shared grid, placed per `WALL_ANCHOR`. WARNING: `RoomDecor` and `RoomShell` are not memoized (TODOS P3); added decor grows the subtree restyled on every row change.

```
 agents(machine) --arrivedAt/leftAt--> door.ts doorOpen --> Scene render
                                          | nextChange          |
                                          v                     v
                        armPaperTimer (min with nextPaper)   RoomDecor: DOOR | DOOR_AJAR + light wedge
 room.ts WALL_LAYOUT/WALL_ANCHOR --> props.ts (BOOKSHELF, PICTURES, shadows, DISPENSER v2)
 room.ts floorCorners(rows) --> robot lane --> scene.css robot-patrol (transform only)
```
